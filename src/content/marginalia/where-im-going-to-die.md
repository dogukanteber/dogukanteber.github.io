## TL;DR

You win mostly by avoiding the predictable stupid mistakes, not by being brilliant: figure out where failure lives and never go there.

## What stuck with me

- **Invert.** Ask what would guarantee failure, then remove those things.
- **Prevention is cheaper than cure.** Build reserves and independence early, so you're never forced into bad decisions.
- **Learn from other people's failures.** Pick up the big mistakes secondhand instead of making them yourself, and once something works, keep doing it.
- **Keep it simple.** Pick easy problems over hard ones, stay inside your circle of competence, protect time to think, and say no to almost everything.
- **Avoid the recipe for misery.** Envy, self-pity, unreliability, and working with people you don't admire.
- **Watch incentives, including your own.** And don't hold an opinion until you can argue the other side better than they can.
