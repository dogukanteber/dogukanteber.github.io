I'm building Nobska, Woodwell's platform for measuring the climate risk of property portfolios.

Analysts upload their properties and see how exposed each one is to climate hazards as the planet warms. So far, it has:

- Risk scores for drought, wildfire, and extreme heat across several warming scenarios, weighted to each team's industry
- A portfolio dashboard and map that show the most exposed properties
- AI-written risk reports
- An API for pulling scores into other tools

## Why it's a milestone

I'm responsible for every part of Nobska, from the design to the deployment and security. It competes with products built by whole teams, and it's a full-stack application that brings together geospatial data and LLM features.
