Each of these skills used to be a mobile app I built from scratch, and most of the work was plumbing: UI, backend, database and deployment. Agents can now do that part themselves, so I shut the apps down and rewrote each one as a folder of instructions and a few scripts.

- [hairmaxxing](https://github.com/dogukanteber/skills/tree/main/hairmaxxing) previews men's haircuts on your own photos before you see a barber.
- [pantry](https://github.com/dogukanteber/skills/tree/main/pantry) tracks the food you have and suggests recipes from it, using Notion as its database.
- [last-call](https://github.com/dogukanteber/skills/tree/main/last-call) clears junk promotional email out of your Gmail inbox every day and learns from what you rescue.

Setup and details are in the [repo](https://github.com/dogukanteber/skills).
