I built the website for Cartolab, Woodwell's cartography studio, where visitors browse the team's maps and request custom ones.

Researchers and partners look through the studio's past work, then request a journal figure, a proposal map, or an editorial piece. Every request lands in an internal dashboard on the same site, so the cartographers can see and manage their inquiries without switching context.
